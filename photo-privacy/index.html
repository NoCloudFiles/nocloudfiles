Where does this information come from, and how do I get the original?
  1. When you share a photo from iPhone, it includes details such as date, location and device by default. You can turn off Location under Options before sharing.
  2. To check the original on a computer, send it with AirDrop and turn on All Photos Data under Options. This sends the original file and its metadata.
  3. Images compressed by chat apps may already have lost some details, so results can differ from the original.

What this check can and cannot do

What it does

  • Shows the metadata found in the original, on this device.
  • Removes all metadata by default, including thumbnails, maker data and unrecognized blocks.
  • Writes only the fields you select, then re-opens the copy to check them.
  • Saves one still JPEG. HDR photos use standard brightness.

What it does not do

  • Hide addresses, faces or anything else visible in the picture itself.
  • Process the paired video of a motion photo.
  • Change your original file.
  • Edit every field. Some specialized fields can only be viewed.

Supports JPEG and HEIC/HEIF still photos up to 32 MB and 50 megapixels.
