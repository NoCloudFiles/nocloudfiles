Can I compress an existing PDF here?

No. Paper Ready turns photos into a PDF. Existing PDFs, Word documents, fillable forms and digital signatures have structures this tool does not preserve. Use the original document when searchable text or signed forms are required.

What if my photos or the final PDF are too large?

Each photo can be up to 20 MB, with 80 MB in total. Resize very high-resolution photos first. If the PDF is still too large at the lowest quality setting, processing stops. Remove unnecessary pages, crop unused edges, or split the submission if permitted. Pages are never silently dropped.
