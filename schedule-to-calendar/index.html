Calendar · Shifts & timetables

Schedule to calendar

Paste a shift list, exam timetable or match schedule. Check each date before saving it to your calendar.

You need
A table with a header row, copied from a spreadsheet or saved as CSV/TSV. See an example
You get
An ICS calendar file and a Google Calendar CSV, after you preview each event
Where files go
Processed in this browser only. Nothing is uploaded; closing the page clears it.
1

Add your schedule

Adding shifts to Apple Calendar? Open this page in Safari before choosing or pasting your schedule. Your table stays in the browser where you entered it.

What should the table look like? 
Title	Date	Time
Night shift	2027-01-31	22:00-06:00
Day off	2027-02-01
  1. The first row holds column headers, then one event per row.
  2. Put date and time in separate columns, or a time range such as 22:00-06:00 in one column. A blank time means an all-day event.
  3. Select the range in Excel or another spreadsheet, including the header row, copy it and paste it into the box above.
2

Match your columns

Times follow the local clock of the destination calendar. A blank time means an all-day event. An overnight shift ends on the next day.


        
Import into a new calendar named Schedule. Check the event count and local times there. If needed, remove that calendar to undo the whole batch.
iPhone: open this page in Safari, then download and open the ICS. Check the number of events at the top and tap Add All. If you only see a file preview, import it on your Mac and sync via iCloud. Android: on a computer, open Google Calendar → Settings → Import & export, then sync the chosen calendar on your phone.
