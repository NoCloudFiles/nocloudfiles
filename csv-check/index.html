      What this check can and cannot fix

      Checks and keeps

      • Checks quoting, column counts and encoding before export.
      • Keeps dates, decimals, spaces and identifiers as written.
      • Exports UTF-8 with BOM, comma separators and consistent quoting.
      • Exports formula-like cells only after you choose how to handle them.

      Does not

      • Repair broken quotes or inconsistent column counts. Correct them before export.
      • Reconstruct missing leading zeros or rounded digits.
      • Check Shopify, Stripe or accounting business rules.
      • Guarantee that a platform accepts the import.

      For platform requirements, consult Shopify CSV import guidance.
