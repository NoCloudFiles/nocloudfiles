Less hassle preparing documents

Free tools that process your files and text in your browser: photos to PDF and CSV check & cleanup.

What it does

Add JPG, PNG, WebP or HEIC photos, arrange and rotate them, optionally crop edges, then generate, preview and download a PDF. Each photo becomes one A4 page. Limits: 20 photos, 20 MB per file, 80 MB total and 36 million pixels per photo. MB means 1,000,000 bytes.

This tool does not edit existing PDFs or Word documents, preserve digital signatures, or provide OCR. The result contains images, not searchable text. Meeting a size limit does not guarantee that a website will accept your documents. Check readability, page order and all submission requirements.

CSV check & cleanup

Checks quoting and column counts, previews cells, and exports a comma-separated UTF-8 CSV with a byte-order mark. Limits: 2 MB, 20,000 records including the first row, 200 columns. Invalid quoting, inconsistent rows and uncertain delimiters block export. Select an encoding explicitly when UTF-8 decoding fails. Formula-like cells require an explicit choice to preserve them or prefix an apostrophe.

We preserve strings, leading zeros and existing precision. We cannot recover values already lost by Excel, infer ambiguous dates, or guarantee acceptance by Shopify, accounting software or any other platform. Import identifiers as text in spreadsheet software. The first row is checked for duplicate or empty names; we do not assume a platform schema. Editing the text area normalizes line endings; unedited file content preserves embedded line endings.

Why these tasks

Upload size limits and multi-step image conversion create friction. This is an experiment to simplify that task, not a claim that existing tools are inadequate. Passing software tests does not prove that this tool has helped real people.

Privacy and optional analytics

Photos, filenames, CSV text and PDF contents stay in your browser. Refreshing or closing the page discards the current work. Cloudflare hosts the static site and processes normal connection information.

Google Analytics loads only if you choose “Allow analytics”. It receives usage events, language, which tool you used and whether you used examples or your own files/text, fixed error categories, and optional feedback categories, plus normal connection information. Photos, filenames, pasted text, document contents, URL queries and free-text feedback are not sent by our analytics code. There are no advertising features. You can decline at any time using “Analytics settings” in the footer. Declining does not affect the tool.

How we assess usefulness

We distinguish sample runs from selected files. Generating a correctly sized PDF and clicking download are separate events; neither proves a successful upload or a readable document. Optional category feedback and independently observed real tasks provide additional evidence. Notes in the feedback box are only copied locally when you ask; they are never transmitted.
