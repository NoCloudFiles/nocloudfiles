Your content stays with you

  • Processed in this browser

    Files and text are read by this page on your device. There is no upload endpoint and no cloud library. You can check this in your browser's developer tools, under Network.

  • Cleared when you leave

    No account. Closing or refreshing a tool clears its work, so download your result first.

  • Analytics only if you allow it

    Optional usage analytics loads only with your permission. It never includes your files, filenames or input text. Declining does not affect the tools.
