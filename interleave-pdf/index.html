    What this tool can and cannot do

    What it does

    • Interleaves front (odd) and back (even) PDF scans into one PDF in reading order.
    • Reverses the back file by default, or keeps it in forward order when needed.
    • Optionally rotates all back pages by 180° while keeping each page's existing rotation.
    • Copies original pages losslessly without rasterizing or re-compressing scans.
    • Flags page-count mismatches and appends trailing pages so no page is silently dropped.

    What it does not do

    • Unlock or read password-protected or encrypted PDFs.
    • Run OCR, deskew crooked scans, crop margins or edit page content.
    • Automatically detect upside-down pages or remove blank back sides.
    • Repair damaged PDFs or split a single mixed PDF into front and back halves.
