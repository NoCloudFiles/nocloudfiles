      What this comparison does and does not do

      Checks and reports

      • Matches rows strictly by the 1 or 2 key columns you select (trimmed exact match).
      • Flags cells wiped from non-empty to blank, plus columns dropped from or added to the updated table.
      • Sums an optional numeric column with exact decimal arithmetic and flags full-column sign flips.
      • Exports a UTF-8 with BOM diff CSV after an explicit choice if formula-like cells appear.

      Does not

      • Guess row matches when keys are blank or duplicated — duplicates and blank keys stop the check with exact record numbers.
      • Coerce currency symbols, thousand-separated numbers, scientific notation or text to 0; unparseable cells are counted separately.
      • Repair broken quotes or inconsistent column counts in either input table.
      • Validate Shopify, ERP, payroll or accounting business rules or guarantee import acceptance.
